[[The Above]]

Sentry dungeons

You now obtain heavy cores exclusively from ominous vaults in sentry dungeons.
Breezes can now only be found spawned from trial spawners in sentry dungeons.

To make up for the mace and breezes being taken out of trial chambers, two new features are added to them:
- **Bores** - Rocky disgruntled earth elementals that look like floating statues with four rods that aim towards their target at all times. Can only move on the XYZ grid. Accelerates on said grid, slamming into you. If they hit you while moving fast enough, they deal heavy damage and ragdoll you. Drop bore rods.
- **Lance** - Crafted from a long needle, obtained exclusively from ominous vaults in trial chambers. Uses a bore rod. Heavy weapon with the ability to hold right click to joust, behaving identically to the spears from newer minecraft versions.
