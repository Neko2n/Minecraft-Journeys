[[The Above]]

The monolith is the obsidian monolith from Bosses of Mass Destruction.

The boss is re-sprited to be made of runestone instead of obsidian, and no longer uses end-related attacks.

The monolith spawns at the top of a tower in a special ruined castle-like structure in [[Wistful Weald]] biomes.

Within this structure there are also evokers, which have been changed to be skeletons instead of illagers.

Defeating this boss is required to obtain the [[Altar]].
