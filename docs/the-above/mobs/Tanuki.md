[[The Above]]

Tanukis are a new passive mob that spawns in [[Twilight Thicket]] biomes, and rarely in [[Golden Woods & Wilds]] woods biomes.

They're little raccoons with two tails. They come in brown and orange.

Tanukis will spin their tails like a helicopter to fly around.

Tanukis can be tamed with [[Cloudberries]].
Tamed tanukis can be picked up and used as a glider which has three "boosts".
Tamed tanukis will automatically attempt to save their owners if they fall into the void, picking them up and boosting upwards.
