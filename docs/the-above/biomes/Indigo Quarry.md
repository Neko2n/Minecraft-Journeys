[[The Above]]

- Generates layered cliffs of indigo stone called rimrock
- Also generates new stone, galena, which can be crafted with two iron nuggets and two rimrock.
- Generates large veins of galena and galena iron ore.
- Broken-up/amplified/fractured worldgen
- Higher concentration of sentry ruins structures
- Gauntlet dungeon can generate
- Unique ruined mineshaft structures
- Geysers which can boost you up between terrain levels, copied from chaos cubed in functionality but with new **kinetic rimrock** instead of potent sulfur, similar to the kinetic basalt found in the Below.
- Generates a bunch of very shallow pools of water
- Generates rare patches of powdered glass and glass formations
- Only place to find levitite for create aeronautics (since it's no longer craftable), which spawns in physical sublevel chunks floating in the air. These sublevels are rimrock platforms or rimrock orbs with levitite in their center, exposed at the bottom.
- Tiny veins of un-subleveled levitite blocks generate buried (cannot be exposed to air)
