[[The Above]]

The basic, most common biomes in the Above.

Both biomes are primarily made of Aspen trees from Nature's Spirit.
Aspen trees can now only be found in the above.

The grass and foliage here--aside from golden flowers and aspen leaves--are all a light warmer green. Not dry like desert colors, but warmer than basic overworld biomes. Closer to a more saturated plains color.

**Golden Woods**
- Kinda just a basic golden forest
- Generates sunflowers, buttercups, and mooblooms
- Has patches of 2-3 block tall grass with a waving animated sprite*
- Generates windwood, a new giant bamboo block which gives off occasional flute sounds and spews air from its top that can lift your glider up
- More mountainous generation
- Spawns tanukis rarely

**Golden Wilds**
- Like golden woods, but with wayyyy fewer trees and flowers and wayyyy more grass
- Does not generate windwood nor spawn tanukis
- Generates slightly more sunflowers than golden woods
- More flat, hilly generation
