[[The Above]]

The wisteria forest biome from Nature's Spirit, transformed to match the atmosphere of the Above.

- Now has heavy magical theming, with new pixie mobs that look like rainbow-colored baby allays, and ancient magical-looking structures covered in runes.
- Generates myalite crystals like the glass dunes (see below)
- Giant wisteria tree structures, which are twilight forest-esque dungeons
- Only biome the runestone monolith boss dungeon can generate in
- Has mist on the ground like dark forests
- The fog here is thicker than in other biomes
