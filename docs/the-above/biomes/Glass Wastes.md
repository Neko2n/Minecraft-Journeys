[[The Above]]

- Made of powdered glass, a powdered snow like block which you sink into without leather boots and very rarely gives off sparkly particles that drift in the wind. While sunken into it, moving deals true damage and inflicts bleeding. Can only be picked up in buckets. Running on this block emits a trail of sparkly glass particles.
- Quickglass, fluid which can only slowly flow down. Behaves like powdered glass but ignores boots. Powdered glass turns into a quickglass source on contact with water.
- Water pools generate in the biome surrounded by quickglass. Water here emits sparkle particles on its surface. Spawns glitterfish, a new sharp fish mob which can't be cooked and inflicts bleeding when eaten.
    - Water next to quickglass is given a special overlay which helps transition between the fluids
- Fiberglass Reeds generate in shallow quickglass and shallow water in this biome. Drops a new material, fiberglass. If broken by a chisel, it will instead drop itself like other plants.
- Glass shards generate sticking out of the ground which have the same effect as powdered glass when touched without boots. Looks like grass made of glass. Can only be collected with silk touch or a scribe.
- During storms, blows sharp glass which inflicts bleeding
- Generates rimrock outcrops as sanctuary from the sand
- Generates crystals of quark's myalite embedded in rimrock
- Higher concentration of sentry ruins structures
- Gauntlet dungeon can generate
