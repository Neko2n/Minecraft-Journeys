[[The Above]]

A new plant called Twine generates on most leafy islands in The Above. Twine is a vine plant that behaves identically to ropes. It can also be used as a caternary, and subsequently, a zipline.

Twine comes in 4 variants:
- Verdant Twine - [[Golden Woods & Wilds]], [[Wistful Weald]], [[Twilight Thicket]]
	- Matches the biome foliage color
- Golden Twine - [[Golden Woods & Wilds]]
	- Same color as aspen leaves
- Flowering Twine - [[Wistful Weald]]
	- Cold blue-green
	- Covered in wisteria
	- Caternaries have a unique dangling wisteria effect
- Dry Twine - [[Twilight Thicket]]
	- Brown and orange, looks sort of dead

Placing twine up against a block will place it like glow lichen, placing a twine carpet block. Placing twine on a twine carpet block which is on the bottom face of a block will create a twine rope that extends downward the more you place twine on it. 

Crouch-clicking a twine carpet block with twine will create a twine caternary. The caternaries generate naturally attached to twine carpets on tree leaves and on the sides of islands.
