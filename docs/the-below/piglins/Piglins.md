[[The Below]]

New: **Reputation System**
Angering or killing piglins drops your reputation. Trading with piglins increases it. When your reputation is low enough, piglins will fight you even if you're wearing gold, and wanted posters will show up in bastions with your player's face on it. 

New: **Piglin Raids**
If your reputation hits zero, you'll be eligible to be attacked by a **new piglin raid event.** Piglins will show up with banners with your face on it crossed out with a red X. They'll swarm you until you either leave the nether or you die.
