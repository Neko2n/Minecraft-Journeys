[[Piglins]]

A giant brass drill car piloted by a piglin engineer that spawns exclusively as the final boss of piglin raids.

Can easily break blocks to get to you and moves faster than your sprinting speed, making it nearly inescapable.

Defeating the pilots will leave behind the excavator, which you can then pilot yourself.

The mech is an aeronautics physics contraption with a special new giant drill multi-block contraption on the front. You can actually mine and move this drill to use it for your own custom setups.
