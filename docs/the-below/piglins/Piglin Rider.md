[[Piglins]]

New piglin enemy that is exclusive to piglin raids.

Piglin riders are just piglin brutes with giant warbanners carrying your bounty poster. They start out by riding armored hoglins, and once you kill the hoglin, they become a regular brute.

Piglin riders will charge up and then dash at you in a straight line for 5 seconds or until they hit a block. Getting hit by the charge deals heavy damage and ragdolls you.
