[[The Below]]

Nether fortresses are overhauled into trial chamber structures with unique trial spawners and trial vaults.

New weapon: the **Claymore**. Obtain a giant blade from ominous vaults in nether fortresses and combine it with a blaze rod to make the claymore.

The claymore's unique effect is that you can hold click to continue swinging by spinning the sword around. This consumes stamina, so you can't do it forever. This makes you immune to knockback and continually does AoE damage around you. Spin damage deals extra knockback to mobs, flinging them away from you.

The claymore's [[Item Awakening]] makes it heat up while spinning. At max heat, the claymore's blade glows with fire, creating a fire tornado that sucks enemies in towards you like a vacuum and lights them on fire.
Innate breach II effect. Innate +2 attack damage.
While in this heated up spinning state, you accrue [[Heat]] at a rate of 0.5 heat per tick.
