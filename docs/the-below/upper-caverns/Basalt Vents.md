[[Upper Caverns]]

Basalt deltas, but with boiling water instead of lava, and generated as caves. 

Generates new geyser blocks, which are a backport of the chaos cubed geysers. Basalt deltas now also generate geysers, which work with lava.
Geysers, instead of potent sulfur, use new **kinetic basalt**, similar to the kinetic rimrock geysers in the Above's Indigo Quarry biome.
