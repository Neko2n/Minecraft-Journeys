[[The Below]]

The upper caverns are a section of the Below from y128 to y256.

Acts as a segway between the overworld and the Below. It's made primarily of deepslate with increasingly common splotches of netherrack going down, as well as **scoria**, a new stone to help blend between deepslate and netherrack.

The upper caverns are full of the remnants of piglin civilization.

The upper caverns are covered in blocks from deeper darker's molten biome, and has the same particle effect in the air. 

Peepers now only spawn here, and now also spawn in the lower caverns. 

Evendeepers spawn here too, but not in the lower caverns.

Has large veins of soul soil and soul slate, and the soul sand valley mobs occasionally spawn here in place of the typical cave undead enemies.

New zombie variant, **The Burned**, which is a dark red zombie that moves slowly but never loses aggro. Also spawns in the lower caverns.

Wither skeletons spawn here in place of regular skeletons sometimes.

New undead mob, **The Boiled**. Looks like a 4-block tall burned with one skinny lanky arm and another big rocky fist. As soon as it spawns it aggros on someone, and they can dig into and out of blocks to teleport. Has a ton of health and hits hard. Can use their big fist to break blocks, and use their long arm to pick up blocks and hurl them at you as dangerous sable sub-levels. Prefers magma blocks, which explode weakly on contact. Also spawns in the lower caverns.

Deepslate quartz ore spawns here frequently, and you can find quartz geodes with special new raw quartz and quartz bud blocks.

New fluid: **boiling water**. Created when water generates naturally in the Below. When bucketed, grants regular water. Has a lighter color than water, and constantly bubbles. Entering it will behave like soul sand columns, and will damage you like lava. Boiling water still evaporates if it somehow reaches the lower caverns.

Blobs of crimson and warped deepslate generate on the surfaces of caves in all upper caverns biomes, which themselves generate the roots and rarely even the trees.

Thorium can generate in the upper caverns, but rarely. It's mostly found in the lower caverns.

Deepslate blobs now generate in the lower caverns, helping drive home that it's a part of the overworld.

Zombified piglins can spawn in the upper caverns rarely, but mostly reside in the lower caverns. Regular piglins cannot spawn in the upper caverns at all.
