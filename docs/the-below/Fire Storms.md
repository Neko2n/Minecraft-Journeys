[[The Below]]

Occasionally, fire will blow through the Below. The fog will get brighter and the intense winds will push you, threatening to ragdoll you if you don't walk against them or take cover behind blocks. Walking against the wind passively consumes stamina, and running out instantly ragdolls you. Knockback resistance scales this effect down, and the effect is completely cancelled out if you have 100% KB resistance. If you're not taking cover, you'll take constant fire damage (but won't be set on fire). Each piece of armor you're wearing reduces the time between damage by 10 ticks. In soul sand valleys, this storm will be blue fire particles. Soul sand blocks will emit sand particles, and rarely soul particles, into the wind. During these storms, flammable blocks will be lit ablaze randomly. The check is run at a 20% chance every 100 ticks, and scans for blocks in a random loaded chunk. Blocks are cached in an array along with a size int. If a block is called to be burned but no longer exists, it will re-roll and remove that block from the cache. This is done multi-threaded. Trading with piglins can get you weighted boots. These are gilded blackstone boots with solid metal bottoms that count as gold armor for piglins. They reduce movement speed by 25%, but grant 100% knockback resistance. During these storms, regular piglins exposed to the open air will now stop in place and brace themselves. Some piglins can spawn with weighted boots, and they will behave as normal. Zombie piglins and all non-Below non-flying mobs will be pushed around by the wind. Non-Below mobs that aren't immune to fire damage will take fire damage.
