[[The Beyond]]

The stronghold from vanilla is removed. The new stronghold is a structure which generates in the Beyond at 0, 0 in place of the removed center island.

The stronghold is an extremely dangerous dungeon akin to a broken-up nether fortress combined with an end city. It's an absolutely massive structure.

One of the rooms in the structure contains a sealed door on the floor, locked behind a special key. This key is obtained from the [[Suspicious Trader]] in exchange for a nether star. If you don't have the key when you reach this room, the suspicious trader will spawn in the room next to you.

Unlocking the sealed door with the key will turn it into a portal to [[The Between]], a dimension which acts as a randomly generated continuation of the stronghold and the final destination of the game.
