[[The Beyond]]

Rifts form all dimensions, and are the primary way of getting to the Beyond. These are random cracks in reality, made up of multiple blocks which replace existing blocks temporarily.

Rifts naturally collapse after roughly half an in-game day, removing the rift and reverting any included blocks to their original versions.

Placing or breaking a block adjacent to a rift block will cause it to collapse immediately.

Rifts have a 50% chance to temporarily convert nearby grass, dirt, stone, and deepslate into endstone, and vice-versa. This conversion is undone when the rift collapses.

Rifts in the overworld, the above, and the below will occasionally spawn endermen and endermites.

Any rifts which generate in the beyond will always warp players back to their spawn point, creating a very short-lived rift in the sky above their spawn point for them to fall out of.

Eyes of ender now create rifts where they fly to, instead of leading to the stronghold (which is now removed). Eyes of ender are one-time use.
