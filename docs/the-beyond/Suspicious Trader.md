The suspicious trader is a tall, cloaked entity that spawns rarely in all dimensions using wandering trader logic, more frequently in [[The Below]] and [[The Beyond]]. In the overworld and [[The Above]], they only spawn early at night or underground, and will de-spawn when exposed to skylight.

The trader sells rare, unique items that can only be obtained from them, as well as other random wares from far-off biomes that the wandering trader wouldn't be able to sensibly get. This includes things like plants from other dimensions, unique mob drops, etc.

The trader always sells a [[Stronghold]] key at the bottom of his trading list, in exchange for a nether star.

The suspicious trader will only spawn once you've gone to [[The Above]] for the first time.
