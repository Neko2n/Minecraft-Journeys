[[The Beyond]]

The between is a dimension accessed exclusively from [[The Stronghold]], and contains the [[Final Boss]].

The between is an infinitely procedurally generated stronghold with more difficult rooms and unique mechanics that only work because it is a dimension.

Once you enter the between, you *cannot leave*. You must defeat the final boss in order to escape.

As you explore the between, chunks behind you will unload and be eligible for new structure placements. This means that you cannot backtrack.

If you die in the between, your items are gone forever, since the chunk they were in is erased. In multiplayer, you can have someone sit near your stuff until you return, but if they leave your stuff or if all players die, it's gone for good.

In multiplayer, the random generation will try to move players towards each other so you don't get permanently lost.

You cannot break the walls of the between, or it will just leak liquid void. The source blocks of liquid void surrounding the stronghold walls will infinitely replace themselves when bucketed.
