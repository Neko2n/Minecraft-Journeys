[[Progression]] [[Early Game]]

The **workbench** is an early-game work station gotten before the crafting table. It is used to combine blocks with items to create new blocks.

The workbench has only two input slots and one output slot, akin to an anvil.
The first input slot accepts only blocks.
The second input slot accepts only non-block items.

The workbench has the following recipes:
- Crafting table - 1 cloth and 1 wood plank
- Furnace - 1 coal and 1 of any stone
- Forge - 1 [[Thermal Shard]] and 1 polished deepslate
	- Will need to be resprited to match
- Smoker - 1 resin and 1 \[log, wood\]
- Blast furnace - 1 iron ingot and 1 polished deepslate
	- Will need to be resprited to match
- Kiln - 1 resin and 1 mud bricks
	- Will need to be resprited to match
- Pottery table - 1 clay ball and 1 wood plank
- Smithing table - 1 steel ingot and 1 polished deepslate
	- Will need to be resprited to match
- Sawmill - 1 iron ingot and 1 wood plank
- Anvil - 1 iron ingot and 1 iron block
- Fletching table - 1 feather and 1 wood plank
- Loom - 1 string and 1 wood plank
- Cartography table - 1 paper and 1 wood plank
- Lectern - 1 book and 1 wood plank
- Altar - 1 diamond and 1 runestone
- Brewing stand - 1 [[Thermal Shard]] and 1 of any stone
- Cooking pot - 1 resin and 1 copper block
