[[Going Above]]

Silver is a tier-2 material that requires an iron pickaxe to mine, and requires hot heat to smelt (see: [[Smelting]])

It is used in many recipes, with its primary use being various tools, utility blocks, and silver armor.

Silver's primary gimmick is magic. It's used in all magic-related items.

Silver weapons deal some of their damage as magic damage, bypassing enemies' defense.
Silver armor gives a unique type of armor which blocks magic damage.
Silver shields can block magic attacks.

Silver is the only resource in the game which comes with an innate +1 enchant slot, allowing for up to three enchantments maximum instead of the usual two.
See: [[Enchanting Overhaul]]

Silver generally has low durability.
