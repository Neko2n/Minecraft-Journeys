[[Going Below]]

Zinc is a tier-3 metal which generates exclusively in [[The Below]] as a liquid. You need to collect it with a bucket, and then empty the bucket in another dimension to get 4 ingots per bucket. The item itself goes from "Bucket of Molten Zinc" to "Bucket of Zinc" when leaving The Below, and vice-versa.

Zinc is used to make [[Brass]].

Zinc ingots can be used to wax oxidizable blocks just like honeycomb.

Zinc ingots can be applied to any item in a smithing table to give it a temporary upgrade equivalent to [[Diamond Upgrading]]. You can do this up to three times for any item. This buff lasts 10 minutes, resetting when you apply a new zinc ingot. The buff drains twice as fast in The Below, lasting only 5 minutes.

Zinc is also a brewing ingredient, providing +3 sour, -2 sweet, +1 strong, and -1 nauseating.

The ingot sprite looks like it's melting, giving it clear resemblance to its fluid properties. This sprite animates in [[The Below]], visibly dripping.
