[[Early Game]]

Lead is an early-game metal which generates in large boulder-shaped veins. These boulders can even sometimes rarely generate on the surface.

Lead requires hot heat to smelt (see: [[Smelting]])

Lead can be crafted into lead rods for use in the [[Assembly Table]] along with numerous other recipes which need a stronger metal stick.

Lead rods can be used to make **lead bolts**, which are stronger crossbow ammo.

Interacting with lead causes **brain damage**. This is relatively unchanged from Oreganized's implementation, though the actual effect is changed to not be a potion effect.

Brain damage is now something closer to temperature.
Going above 50 brain damage turns your stamina bar purple and applies a debuff to regen/drain.
Going above 75 brain damage turns your hearts purple, disabling all health regen. You also get a weaker version of the nausea wobble effect.
Hitting 100 brain damage will break one of your hearts, apply 10% stamina wither, and reset your brain damage to 80.
Brain damage takes a long time to deplete.
