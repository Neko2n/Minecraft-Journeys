[[Progression]] [[Early Game]]

Beacon shrines are fairly rare structures which generate on the surface of the overworld. One is always guaranteed to spawn exactly 16 chunks away from world spawn.

In the four corners of each beacon shrine are pedestals with diamond icons etched into their sides. Place a diamond on each pedestal to activate it, channeling runes into the beacon at the center of the shrine. Once all four pedestals are activated, the beacon will begin to charge.

This beacon requires time to charge up. In this time, four **beacon golems** will awaken around the shrine to seek out and destroy the diamonds. They awaken one at a time every 30 seconds. Players must defeat the four golems before the beacon will fully charge.

**On hard mode**, upon being defeated, a beacon golem will just be downed, and the beacon in the center will channel runes into it. After 30 seconds, the golem will just get back up. You need to defeat them all simultaneously to win.

Once the beacon is fully charged, it will explode with light and get twice as wide. It will change from the default light blue to a bright gold.

The fully charged beacon will slowly burn through blocks that are in front of it, breaking them with special bright gold emmissive cracks.

The beacon burns undead mobs that get near it, even when they're not exposed to sunlight.

Stepping into a fully charged beacon will begin to accelerate you upwards. Once you hit a certain altitude, you'll be warped to [[The Above]].
