Armor and armor toughness are now overhauled into two new systems which are heavily reliant on the [[Health & Damage Overhaul]].

**Basic armor** grants segments to a new armor bar which absorbs a certain number of hits before shattering, refilling, and taking one of your hearts.

**Shields** are the replacement for toughness. They are basically one-time-use extra hearts. Once you lose a shield heart, it's gone until you repair the armor piece. Repairing an armor piece once immediately refills all of its shield hearts. Shield hearts are overlaid on top of your regular hearts starting from your first heart.
Armor with broken shield hearts will show a broken shield heart icon in its tooltip.

[[Heavy Attacks]] will immediately shatter your armor bar regardless of how much armor you have, dealing a guaranteed one heart of damage. They will also temporarily disable your armor bar with an icon of the weapon that caused it, preventing it from refilling and leaving you exposed to taking two full hearts of double damage.

[[True Attacks]] will ignore your armor bar, reducing your hearts and leaving your armor unaffected. This includes stuff like harming potions, fall damage, heat damage, dragon breath, etc.

Explosion damage can be heavier than heavy attacks. They still behave like heavy attacks in that they'll do one less heart of damage and shatter your armor, but say they would have done five hearts of damage, it will instead do four.

On mobs, armor still works identically to how it did before, using armor to calculate defense and using shields to calculate toughness.
