Damage is now dealt in full hearts. There are no half hearts, and there's no fractional damage that deals less than that.

Almost all damage sources now deal one full heart on contact.

Your hearts can now be damaged in multiple ways.
There's regular damage, and then theres:
- Broken hearts - cannot be regenerated. Broken hearts repair at a rate of one heart every 10 minutes. Sleeping removes one broken heart. Broken hearts persist through death.
- Withered hearts - like broken hearts, but clears after just 2 minutes from when it was applied, and resets on death. Replaces the wither potion effect.

Some damage sources are deemed as **Heavy Attacks**. This includes any mob with a weapon, warden sonic booms, large mobs like golems, etc.

Some damage sources are even heavier than heavy attacks, being able to deal more than two hearts of damage. This includes only explosions and warden melee attacks. These attacks will *always* break at least one heart.

Some damage sources are deemed as **True Attacks**. These damage sources can deal infinitely many hearts of damage, and ignore armor (see: [[Armor Overhaul]]). This includes magic damage, fall damage, and heat damage.

**Immunity Frames**
To compensate for this new system, you have 40 ticks of immunity frames against all regular and heavy damage.
If you lose a heart that hit, your immunity frames double to 80.
True damage and explosion damage bypasses iframes entirely.

**Fire Damage**
[[Heat]] is a new damage system to handle fire damage.

**Poison Effect**
Poison now accrues like heat or freeze, going from 0 to 100. Once it hits 100, it resets back to 0. The accrual rate is N poison per tick, where N is the level of the effect.

**Absorption Effect & Overhealth**
Absorption as a potion effect is changed to provide golden shields instead of golden hearts, which coat the hearts at the *end* of your health bar, unlike armor shields which coat the hearts at the beginning. See: [[Armor Overhaul]]
This results in the absorption effect being functionally identical to vanilla.
A new mechanic called **overhealth** is added which is functionally just absorption without a time limit. Overhealth renders as golden hearts at the end of your health bar.
Currently, there are only two planned ways of getting overhealth:
- Sleeping grants +1 overhealth
- Warm Water grants +1 overhealth (see: [[Bubbles & Hot Tubs]])
