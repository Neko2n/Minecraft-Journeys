### Overview

Hunger is changed to be a "delayed healing currency". You eat something, it gives you shanks, and roughly every 2 minutes, a shank will be consumed to grant an effect.

When you're out of hunger, your stamina is debuffed, and you'll take one heart of damage every minute down to a minimum of one heart.

Hunger drain will pause if you don't move for over 60 seconds.

You can no longer heal damaged hearts directly from things like potions and bandages. Potions are removed from the game entirely anyway, and bandages are now only used to heal bleeding hearts (see: [[Health & Damage Overhaul]]).

There are six types of hunger that can be granted from food:
- Regular shanks - heals 1 heart when consumed. No passive effects.
- Golden shanks - heals 1 heart and grants 1 golden heart when consumed. Passively buffs your stamina while active.
- Large shanks - heals 1 heart when consumed. No passive effects. Upon being consumed, turns into a regular shank instead of disappearing.
- Sickly/poison shanks - does nothing when consumed. Passively debuffs your stamina while active. Typically gotten from raw meats and rotten foods. Only takes 1 minute to be consumed instead of 2 minutes.
- Hot shanks - heals 1 heart when consumed. Passively increases your temperature while active.
- Cold shanks - heals 1 heart when consumed. Passively decreases your temperature while active.
Drinking milk converts all non-regular shanks into regular shanks. It no longer clears potion effects.
You can have at most 5 shanks. This means your hunger typically drains to empty every 10 minutes.

### Resting

You can now click a "rest" button that shows above campfires and beds to rest, skipping a bit of time and directly spending hunger shanks to heal. This rest button only shows when you have a "Zzz" icon above your character head in the middle of your hotbar; you cannot rest otherwise. The "tired" state by default takes 24k ticks to reset, but it drains with your stamina too, so being active makes you tired. The tired state is disabled when your character is in combat or in a dangerous place (eg. the deep dark, the below).

Resting at a campfire or a straw bed takes 1 hunger for 1 heart, only healing at most 5 hearts. It skips 1k ticks per hunger spent.
Resting at a bed takes 1 hunger for 2 hearts, healing at most 10 hearts (maximum possible health). It skips 2k ticks per hunger spent. It also gives you a golden heart.
Beds are now far more expensive mid-game items, requiring a new cloth item. Cloth is crafted from processing wool in a new loom crafting station which requires kinetic rotation (the hand crank by default works for this).

Resting always heals one broken heart, if you have any. See: [[Health & Damage Overhaul]]


### Items

Food doesn't stack. New lunchbox item which holds up to 6 food items in one slot. New basket item which holds up to 64 of one type of non-bowl food.

Food items now rot. See: [[Rotten Food, Salt, & Iceboxes]].


### Misc

Sprinting no longer requires food, instead requiring [[Stamina]].
