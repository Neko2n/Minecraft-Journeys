Freezing is an overhaul to the vanilla freezing effect to have it play better with the [[Health & Damage Overhaul]], as well as to allow it to function as an environmental effect.

Freezing no longer deals health damage. Instead, freezing will just freeze your hearts, not allowing you to regenerate from any sources.

Freezing will also turn your stamina bar a bright frosty blue, completely disabling its regen.

The creeping freezing vignette is replaced with a subtler blue gradient. The vanilla gradient still pops back in when you're at 100 freeze / completely frozen.

Freeze is gained from the following sources:
- Being in cold biomes gives freeze at a rate of 0.5 freeze per tick, but caps out at 50 freeze.
- Being in powdered snow gives freeze at a rate of 1 freeze per tick.
- Being near soul fire / soul campfires gives freeze scaling with how close you are, but caps out at 50 freeze.
- Being near ectoplasm gives freeze scaling with how close you are, but caps out at 90 freeze when you're as close as possible without being in it.
- Being in ectoplasm or soul fire gives 5 freeze per tick.
- Getting hit by soul-related enemies gives 20 freeze on regular hits, 80 freeze on heavy hits

Freeze is the direct inverse of [[Heat]]. Gaining one will reduce the other, and vice versa. They're handled by the same innate temperature system.
