
Enchanting as it's known is completely removed, replaced with the following:

[[Diamond Upgrading]]
Basic raw stat upgrades that apply to all equipment items, replacing enchantments like unbreaking and sharpness.

[[Item Awakening]]
Grants items direct upgrades that aren't just stats, replacing enchantments like breach and quick draw.
This is also the way to give items the enchanted foil overlay effect.

[[Affixes]]
Unique items can be attached to armor and tools to give them special capabilities, and a little extra visual flair.

[[Chisel]]
Multi-tool replacement for silk touch.

**Unbreakable Equipment**
Replacements for mending. Includes:
- [[Zinc]] application
- **Master Smithing Upgrades** via. [[Tungsten]]
- [[Sanguine]] armor
