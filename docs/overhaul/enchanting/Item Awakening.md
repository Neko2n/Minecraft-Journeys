[[Enchanting Overhaul]] [[The Beyond]] [[Stronghold]]

In the stronghold is a single, special room containing a giant glowing purple tree.

At the foot of the tree is a unique crafting setup involving four special brazier podiums with unique purple fire surrounding a unique endstone version of the [[Altar]].

The altar only accepts equipment items. To show this, the altar has the sword, axe, pickaxe, and shovel inscribed into its sides. It also rejects non-equipment items immediately, while equipment items are sort of "locked in" and begin glowing purple once placed.

The brazier podiums need to be lit by igniting four wayfinder braziers (those big braziers from The Beyond mod) found throughout the stronghold.
Each one is in a room requiring you to defeat a gauntlet of mobs. Once the mobs are defeated, the braziers are automatically lit.
These braziers also have the same unique purple fire.

You only need to do this once. Braziers stay lit permanently once lit.

Once all four braziers are lit and an equipment item is placed on the altar, the braziers will channel purple fire into the equipment item for roughly 20 seconds. During this process, the altar will glow violently and enchanting runes will swirl around it.

After the process is finished, the item will be **awakened**.

Awakened items gain the enchanted foil overlay effect, a purple name, and are granted a unique stat upgrade depending on what the item was.

Item awakening upgrades are as follows:
- [[Spears]] / trident - Loyalty & +1 attack damage
- Bow - Quick draw II enchantment effect
- Crossbow - Multishot II enchantment effect
- Sword - Parrying makes your sword glow, guaranteeing a critical hit on the next swing if it lands. Innate +1 attack damage.
- Axe - Critical hits now cripple opponents and deal double knockback. Innate +2 attack damage. Chopping trees down now breaks the entire tree at once, unless you're crouching.
- Shovel - Partially damages blocks around the target block to mine in a 3x3, unless you're crouching.
- Pickaxe - Vein mining effect that spreads partial damage through ore veins to help break them in at most a few hits.
- Onyx Drill - See: [[Onyx]]
- Shield - Will now glow on block, allowing you to click attack to shield bash, dealing a guaranteed critical hit with your held weapon and flinging the enemy away with high knockback. Innate shield stat improvement bonus.
- Mace - Now creates a scaling AoE wind explosion on successful smash attacks which launches you back up. The mace glows brighter blue the stronger your smash attack is going to be / the faster you're falling. Innate density II and breach II effects. Innate +2 attack damage.
- Claymore - See: [[Nether Fortress Revamp]]
- Lance - Now accelerates your sprinting speed while jousting. Jousting is guaranteed to crit above a certain speed, denoted by your lance's glow maxing out. Innate breach II effect. Innate +2 attack damage.
	- See: [[Sentry Dungeons]] (found in trial chambers since the mace moved)
- Armor - Gets an extra +1 shield.
