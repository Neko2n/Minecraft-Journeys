[[Enchanting Overhaul]]

Apply a diamond to any equipment in a smithing table to increase its stats.

Each diamond is:
- Weapons: +20% damage, +50% durability
- Tools: +50% durability, +1 mining speed
- Armor: +50% durability, +1 shield (see: [[Armor Overhaul]])

Once you have access to an [[Altar]], you can create **Enchanted Diamonds**, which can be further applied to give your items another three upgrades.

Enchanted diamonds are made by infusing a diamond with 4 [[Enchanted Gems]].
