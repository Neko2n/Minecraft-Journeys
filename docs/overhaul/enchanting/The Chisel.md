[[Enchanting Overhaul]]

Silk touch is removed, replaced with a dedicated tool, the **Chisel**.

Mines glass-like blocks faster (including the new obsidian)

Can mine any block at the tier of a diamond pickaxe with the speed of a stone tool.

Found in ancient cities and through archaeology.

Appears to be made out of obsidian, and can be repaired with [[Obsidian Shards]].
