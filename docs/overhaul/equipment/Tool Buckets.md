
Buckets no longer stack.

There are now three types of buckets: ceramic buckets, iron buckets, and tungsten buckets. All buckets are now cube-shaped.

A ceramic bucket can hold up to 2 blocks of a fluid. It has 64 durability. While holding lava, the bucket glows and loses 1 durability every 2 seconds that it is in your inventory. If it breaks while holding lava, a lava source block is placed directly on your player.

An iron bucket can hold up to 4 blocks of a fluid. It has 512 durability. While holding lava, the bucket glows and loses 1 durability every 4 seconds that it is in your inventory. If it breaks while holding lava, a lava source block is placed directly on your player.

A tungsten bucket can hold up to 4 blocks of a fluid. It has 2048 durability. It can hold any fluid without issue.

Vanilla buckets which can capture animals are replaced with **fluid barrels**. Barrel blocks are given the functionality of vanilla buckets, and can now store fluids, which turns them into an unstackable item (identically to vanilla buckets). Barrels cannot carry lava. Fluid barrels can be placed with shift + click, and are identical in functionality to cauldrons.
