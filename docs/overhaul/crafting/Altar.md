[[Going Above]]

The altar is a tier-2 work station crafted with runestone, a block gotten exclusively from defeating the [[Monolith]] boss.

The altar is used to infuse multiple items into one center item via. a magical ritual.

The altar also has the ability to summon mobs through certain recipes, all of which involve essence bottles (experience bottles)

The altar's primary uses are [[Rune Abilities]] and crafting [[Mithril]], as well as just generally being used for all metaphysical crafting recipes.
