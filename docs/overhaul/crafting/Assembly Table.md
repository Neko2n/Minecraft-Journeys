[[Early Game]]

The **Assembly Table** is an early-game work station used to craft functional blocks.

Its UI is a 3-tall grid of inputs leading to 1 output. Its recipes are exclusively shaped.
It also has one slot on the left that accepts the [[Hammer & Wrench]].

Some example recipes:
- 3 iron ingots, with a hammer = iron rod
- 2 iron ingots + 1 iron rod (in the middle), with a hammer = cogwheel
- 1 iron block on the bottom, 1 iron rod above that, 1 cogwheel above that, with a wrench = mechanical press
- 2 wood planks + 1 iron rod (in the middle), with a hammer = waterwheel
- 2 redstone + 1 wood plank (in the middle), with a wrench = noteblock
- 2 spinel + 1 wood plank (in the middle), with a wrench = jukebox
- 2 cobblestone + 1 bow (in the middle), with a wrench = dispenser
