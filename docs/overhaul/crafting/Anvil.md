[[Early Game]]

The anvil is now an A + B = C crafting station which requires a hammer.

Like the forge, the anvil's crafting output requires clicking a hammer button. The button is blocked if you don't have a hammer in the hammer slot. Crafting takes a short time akin to the mortar.

The "B" slot of the anvil has a counter underneath it. This is the required number of items to put in the B slot to make the recipe. Various recipes require differing amounts of items in said slot.

The anvil is most notably used for upgrading leather armor into metal armors using metal plates, and for upgrading tools and armor with their respective materials to increase their stats.

Upgrading is done in 3 stages:
- 4 of the material -> +1
- 5 of the material -> +2
- 6 of the material -> +3
Taken from Wayfarer

Metal armor is crafted by:
- Leather helmet + 5 metal plates = metal helmet
- Leather chestplate + 8 metal plates = metal chestplate
- Leather leggings + 7 metal plates = metal leggings
- Leather boots + 4 metal plates = metal boots
