[[Early Game]]

The research slot is a slot in your inventory where the recipe book used to be, which is used for seeing how items can be used. The slot has a magnifying glass icon.

Once an item has been placed in the slot, information shows on the left where the recipe book once was. This info displays which crafting stations the item can be used in as well as which items they're used with in said crafting stations.

Any items or crafting stations shown which have not yet been obtained or interacted with before by your player in the given world, will be shown as a silhouette. Their name will be replaced by "???" and their tooltip description will be the word "Undiscovered" in red. These items and stations will also be sorted down to the bottom of the lists.

There's also a counter for "recipes discovered" which shows the total number of recipes that item is used in for the selected work station, and the number of recipes for that work station which you've crafted before, as a fraction. This is to let you know when you have recipes yet to discover. Crafting recipes for the first time will show a brief notification in the top right of your screen with a lightbulb saying "recipe discovered".
