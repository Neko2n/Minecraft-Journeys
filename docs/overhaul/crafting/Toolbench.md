[[Early Game]]

The toolbench is a crafting station made in a workbench with two leather and two wooden planks.

The toolbench has a circle of 7 hexagon-shaped input slots which lead to one output slot.

All recipes in the toolbench can be freely rotated.

The toolbench doesn't use traditional recipes. It uses shapes which request two types of items: sticks, and heads.
Sticks are twigs, handles, and lead rods. A tool's stick improves its durability. Lead rods have the highest durability, but lower the tool's speed.
Heads are whatever material is making the tool. For instance, bones will make bone tools. Stone will make stone tools.
This essentially functions like tinker's construct. All tools use the same item ID, with their NBT determining what they're made of and what their stats are.

Some key recipes:
- 3 head 2 stick -> pickaxe
- 2 head 2 stick -> axe
- 1 head 2 stick -> shovel
- 2 head 1 stick -> sword
- 1 head 1 stick -> knife


**Blueprints**

The toolbench has a blueprint slot which is used for crafting special tools.
Planned blueprints:
- Ancient Paxel blueprint
	- 3 ancient scrap 2 handles
	- Obtained from dungeons in the below
- Crude Shield blueprint
	- 6 planks around 1 handle
	- Obtained from dungeons underground
	- Cannot block explosions, instantly breaks against axes, loses 4x durability from blocking fire damage
- Tower Shield blueprint
	- 6 platinum plates around 1 handle
	- Obtained from dungeons in the above
	- Drastically slowed movement speed while blocking
- Mace blueprint
	- 1 lead block 2 breeze rods
	- Obtained from dungeons in the above

	- 1 quartz 1 arcane focus 1 breeze rod
	- Obtained from dungeons in the above
	- Shoots wind charges. See: [[Magic Wands]]

	- 1 citrine 1 arcane focus 1 blaze rod
	- Obtained from dungeons in the below
	- Shoots fire charges. See: [[Magic Wands]]
