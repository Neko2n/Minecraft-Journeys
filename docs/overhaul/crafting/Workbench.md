[[Early Game]]

The **workbench** is an early-game work station gotten before the crafting table.

It has a 2x2 grid like the old player inventory.

The workbench is solely used to make work stations.

The workbench has the following recipes:
- Crafting table - 1 leather and 1 treated wood
- Furnace - 1 coal and 1 of any stone
- Smoker - 1 resin and 1 \[log, wood\]
- Blast furnace - 1 thermal shard and 1 polished deepslate
	- Will need to be resprited to match
- Stonecutter - 1 iron ingot and 1 of any stone
- [[Assembly Table]] - 1 gold ingot and 1 treated wood
- Kiln - 1 resin and 1 bricks
	- Will need to be resprited to match
- Pottery table - 1 clay ball and 1 treated wood
- Smithing table - 1 lead ingot and 1 treated wood
	- Will need to be resprited
- Sawmill - 1 iron ingot and 1 treated wood
- Anvil - 1 iron ingot and 1 iron block
- Fletching table - 1 feather and 1 treated wood
- Loom - 1 string and 1 treated wood
- Cartography table - 1 paper and 1 treated wood
- Lectern - 1 book and 1 treated wood
- [[Altar]] - 1 silver ingot and 1 runestone
- [[Brewing stand]] - 1 thermal shard and 1 silver block
- [[Cooking pot]] - 1 resin and 1 copper block
- [[Crucible]] - 1 tungsten ingot and 1 scoria
