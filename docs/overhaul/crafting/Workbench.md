[[Early Game]]

The **workbench** is an early-game work station gotten before the crafting table.

It has a 2x2 grid like the old player inventory.

The workbench is used for almost all recipes in the game. It's basically the crafting table, but a 2x2 grid is simpler and easier to work with.

Some crucial recipes:
- 2 logs + 2 rope = raft
- 2 planks + 2 leather = [[Toolbench]]
- 4 planks = chest
- 4 treated planks = barrel
- 1 barrel + 1 water bucket = [[Vat]]
	- Leaves behind the empty bucket
- 4 cobblestone = furnace
- 4 bricks = kiln
- 2 hardstone above 2 planks = [[Mortar & Millstone | Mortar]]
- 1 hardstone above 1 plank = [[Grindstone]]
- 4 hardstone = stone [[Anvil]]
- 2 iron blocks above 2 iron ingots = [[Anvil]]
- 1 spoon above 2 copper ingots next to 1 water bucket = [[Cooking Pot]]
	- Leaves behind the empty bucket
- 1 lava bucket above 2 deepslate next to 1 thermite = [[Forge]]
- 2 \[handle, twig\] above 2 clay balls = unfired ceramic bucket
	- Smelt a clay bucket in a furnace on any heat to get a ceramic bucket
- 2 copper ingots above 2 planks = [[Assembly Table]]
- 2 flint above 2 planks = [[Fletching Table]]

All inventory recipes also work in the workbench as shapeless recipes.
