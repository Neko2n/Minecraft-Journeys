
Smelting is overhauled to use three different tiers of heat, allowing different tiers of progression to be gated behind fuel materials.

Tiers are as follows:
- Tier 1 - Warm. Fire is a dull red-orange. Items take twice as long to smelt.
- Tier 2 - Hot. Fire is a bright yellow-orange. Items take regular time to smelt.
- Tier 3 - Blazing. Fire is a bright cyan. Items take half the time to smelt.

Fuels are as follows:
- Coal, wood, foliage - Warm
- [[Thermal Shard]], lava, blaze rods - Hot
- [[Thorium]] - Blazing

Attempting to smelt an item when you don't have enough heat will put an X over the progress arrow and the fire icon will quiver side to side. Hovering over either one will display the tooltip "Not enough heat!" in red text.

Requirements are as follows:
- Warm - Copper, silver, zinc, anything else not listed
- Hot - Iron, gold
- Blazing - Tungsten, ancient scrap, tin
