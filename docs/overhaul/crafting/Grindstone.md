[[Early Game]]

The grindstone doesn't have a UI. Rather, you click it with an item to transform that item into something else.

The grindstone is most notably used for making blades, as well as for sharpening tools.

You can make iron blades by clicking a grindstone with an iron ingot. You can make platinum blades by clicking the grindstone with a platinum ingot. You can make titanium blades by clicking the grindstone with a titanium ingot.

Grindstones are crafted by putting a [[Hardstone]] on top of a plank in a workbench.

**Tool Sharpening**
Click a grindstone with a tool to give the tool the "sharpened" buff, which gives it +1 damage and +1 mining speed until the next time you repair it.
