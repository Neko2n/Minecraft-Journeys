[[Early Game]]

The smithing table is an early-game work station used to upgrade tools and apply decorative tool/armor trims.

Smithing anything requires a template. There are three different types of upgrade templates you can find:
- Basic Upgrade Template
	- Apply diamonds to your gear
	- Found in an item frame in special forge structures in deepslate layer
- Ascendant Upgrade Template
	- Apply black diamonds to your gear to grant it TBD
	- Found in an item frame in sentry dungeons
- Eldritch Upgrade Template
	- Apply black diamonds to your gear to grant it TBD
	- Found in an item frame in ancient cities
- Infernal Upgrade Template
	- Apply black diamonds to your gear to grant it TBD
	- Found in an item frame in sanctums

Trim templates and upgrade templates are **no longer consumed on use**. They're also no longer duplicated with diamonds.
To compensate for this change, trim and upgrade templates are really rare.
Templates now only stack to 1.
