[[Early Game]]

The fletching table is given a crafting interface used to make arrows.

The interface has 3 input slots aligned vertically; one tip slot, one stick slot, and one feather slot.

All ammunition recipes are moved to be fletching table exclusive, with the exception of modification recipes like tipped arrows and incendiary arrows.

The fletching table has the following recipes:
- Flint, stick, feather = 4 flint arrows
- Bone, stick, feather = 4 bone arrows
- Pebble, stick, feather = 4 stone arrows
- Obsidian shard, stick, feather = 4 obsidian arrows (pierces once, immune to fire)
- Lead ingot, lead rod, feather = 4 lead bolts (crossbow exclusive)
- Onyx, bore rod, feather = 4 drill arrows (breaks blocks and stuns enemies)
- Amethyst shard, breeze rod, feather = 4 magic arrows
	- Ignores gravity
	- Leaves behind magic particles as it travels
	- Weakly homes in on enemies
- Thermal shard, blaze rod, feather = 4 thermal arrows (weak explosion on hit)
- Phasmo shard, bone, feather = 4 spectral arrows (goes through blocks and infinitely pierces)
- Tungsten, lead rod, feather = 4 heavy bolts (crossbow exclusive)
	- Vastly reduced fire velocity, but damage scales with downward velocity.
	- Makes an anvil sound upon hitting the ground and cracks the block that it hit. If it was traveling fast enough, it stuns enemies.
	- Mostly a joke item; the sprite is literally just a tungsten cube on a stick.
