[[Going Below]]

The **crucible** is a tier-3 work station used to combine ores into new ones.

Fueling the crucible requires [[Thorium]]. It doesn't accept any other fuel.

The interface has four input slots lined up horizontally, with fluid lines leading to one output slot below them. The input slots have ingot icons, and only accept metal ingots.
There's a fuel slot below all of this at the bottom of the GUI, with an icon matching the thorium item sprite.

Crucible recipes take 16x longer than furnace recipes, consuming 1 thorium each craft.

The crucible has the following recipes:
- 2 gold and 2 zinc = 1 [[Brass]]
- 2 iron, 1 diamond powder, and 1 coke coal = 1 [[Steel]]
- 2 copper and 2 tin = 1 bronze, used for niche decorative blocks like the bronze bell

Crucibles can be found in Bastion workshops as a form of world building, to explain how Piglins make their brass.

The crucible is crafted with 1 scoria and 1 [[Titanium]]. It takes a long time to break and requires a tier-3 tool or mechanical drill to mine.
