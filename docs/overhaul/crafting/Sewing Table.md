[[Early Game]]

The sewing table is a 3x3 grid work station used to make clothing items.

There's a dropdown on the left for selecting a shape which gives you a hint on where to put items. The shapes are helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots by default, but you can find "sewing patterns" in the world and add them to a 9x1 inventory in the bottom of the sewing table UI to unlock more shapes (unique recipes).

Some basic recipes:
- Leather armor
- Grass armor
- Wool clothes
	- Increases your temperature
	- Gives no protection
