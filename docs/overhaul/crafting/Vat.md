[[Early Game]]

Basically a wooden cauldron.

All recipes which previously used dye + one other item are ported to the vat. 

The vat has a fluid gauge that determines what fluid it holds. The gauge has 8 charges, allowing you to process 8 items with 1 fluid item.

The vat allows you to use dyes efficiently. If the vat is full of water and you add a dye, the water will be dyed, and then you can process items with it. Empty water will remove dye from items.

You can put wax in the vat like a fluid to craft wax recipes more efficiently.
Some example recipes:
- Rawhide -> leather, costs 4 charges (2x efficiency)
- String -> candle, costs 4 charges (2x efficiency)
- Planks -> treated planks, costs 1 charge (8x efficiency)
- (any wax-able block) -> (waxed variant), costs 1 charge (8x efficiency)

Vats above a heat source will bubble like cauldrons.

You can add fluids/wax/dyes directly to a vat by crouch+clicking it with the item in hand.

You can directly process items with a filled vat by crouch+clicking the filled vat with the item in hand.
