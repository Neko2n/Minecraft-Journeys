All illager features are merged into existing areas of the game.

Raids aren't really that fun, so I'm mostly just removing them, but the piglin raids are a similar mechanic.

Totem of undying - gotten from ancient cities on a pedestal in front of the portal. Under the pedestal is an alarm trap that rings when you take the totem off. Re-sprited to look more like the ancient people than villagers. Color palette is swapped from gold and emerald to deepslate and sculk.

Evokers - changed to be new skeleton mobs which generate exclusively as pre-placed mobs in runestone monolith dungeons. See: [[Monolith]]

Vexes - Changed to wield a silver sword instead of an iron sword.
