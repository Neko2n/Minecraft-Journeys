Typical mob spawners are removed.
Two new custom spawning blocks are added for elementals and golems.

Elemental spawners are for blazes, breezes, bores, and brines. They're a crystal entity with enchant runes on it, much like end crystals. They have an initial shield that requires killing 10 of its corresponding elementals to break, after which you can hit the crystal a few times to shatter it. The crystal regenerates this shield after 2 irl minutes (resets on hit), and regenerates any cracks from hitting it every 5 irl seconds (resets on hit).
Blaze crystals explode into fire when broken, dealing high damage to nearby entities and the terrain. Breeze crystals explode into wind, knocking you really high up and far away. Bore crystals explode into painful shards of rock.

Golem spawners are a new block which summons golems from their surrounding blocks. They're a traditional spawner block that function identically the same, but they take a really long time to break. They're made of stone and have swirling souls in their center which are imbued into the blocks to form the golems. 
Breaking a golem spawner drops a **soul stone** item, which is the new required item for spawning golems instead of pumpkins.
Different golem spawners pull from different block types to spawn different golems:
- Stone golem spawners pull from all stones to spawn generic humanoid golem entities which are basically just stronger zombies
- Nether brick golem spawners pull from surrounding netherrack, soul sand, etc. to spawn various ancient knightly constructs wielding weapons
- Floatstone golem spawners pull from surrounding floatstone and rimrock to spawn various ancient tech golems
Golem entities only really drop their respective blocks when killed, so golem spawners are never worth farming and always better off being broken for a soul stone.
Clicking a valid block with a soul stone consumes the soul stone to summon a loyal golem out of the block. 

Other miscellaneous mob spawner blocks:
- Grave soil - spawns undead mobs like zombies and skeletons directly on top of it. Generates everywhere in the overworld and the below, though very rarely on the surface of the overworld and mostly in ruined village structures.
- Spider nests -  passively spawn spiders that crawl out of it when players are nearby. Three types:
  - Common - logs that generate in trees on the surface of the overworld. Spawns spiderlings and regular spiders. 
  - Cave - generates in stone and deepslate. spawns cave spiders. more common in mineshafts, where there are certain "infested" rooms absolutely full of these nest blocks.
  - Dweller - generates in deepslate. spawns dweller spiders. very common. only generates when its bottom face is exposed to air, and dwellers which spawn from it are always dropped from the block's bottom face.
- Hunt Den - multiblock that spawns up to 5 hunts which are loyal to the den. Sometimes generates with an alpha hunt guarding the den. Killing the hunts will allow the den to periodically try to respawn them. Breaking the den drops a bunch of old animal scraps and typical hunt drops.
