
Bubble columns can now be created by three more blocks.

**Kinetic Basalt** and **Kinetic Rimrock** are new blocks which produce upwards bubble columns in a 3x3 around them. These bubble columns are weaker than soul sand columns.
These blocks also rarely spawn geysers directly above them, which are ported in functionality from Chaos Cubed.
Kinetic Basalt generates in [[Basalt Vents]] and Basalt Deltas biomes.
Kinetic Rimrock generates in [[Indigo Quarry]] biomes.

**Sunstone** is a new class of block that generates in [[The Above]]. 
It gives off a light level of 2.
Touching sunstone directly gives [[Heat]] at a rate of 0.5 heat per tick, up to a maximum of 20 heat.
All sunstone blocks produce upward bubble columns. These columns are extremely weak, and cannot push entities larger than a chicken.
Standing in sunstone-derived bubble columns applies [[Heat]] at a rate of 0.25 heat per tick, up to a maximum of 40 heat.

**Hot Tubs**
Standing in water affected by sunstone will very slowly regenerate your health. This is shown by a little bubble icon with a bobbing green up arrow next to your hearts bar, as well as your hearts doing the little vanilla left-to-right wave animation.
This regeneration effect gets stronger every 100 ticks that you're in the water, up to a maximum of four stacks.
Regeneration from hot tubs can exceed your maximum health, providing you 1 overhealth. See: [[Health & Damage Overhaul]]
