Obsidian now breaks into shards instead of the block.

Makes a deeper glass break sound when broken.

The shards are immune to fire and explosion damage, behaving identically to the block.

Collecting the block directly requires [[The Chisel]].

Obsidian shards are used to craft the spear and knife.

Many recipes which previously used obsidian blocks now use obsidian shards, if the result isn't block-related.
