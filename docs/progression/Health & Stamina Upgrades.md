[[Progression]]

As you progress, you'll be able to obtain permanent upgrades to your health and stamina.

### Stamina Upgrades

Stamina gets **4** permanent upgrades, one for each dimension.
This brings your base stamina from 1 to 5.

Stamina upgrades are obtained from **Sacred Springs**. Each dimension has exactly one sacred spring structure, and it generates somewhere within 1000-2000 blocks of 0, 0. The one in the beyond is a room of the stronghold.

Sacred springs can be found by special wayfinder structures. They're all made from the same unique stone as the springs are. These structures generate with fingers pointing toward the springs.
These wayfinder statues are the same as the ones in the beyond that point to the stronghold.

To increase your stamina, simply touch the special glowing essence liquid that makes up the spring. You can bucket this essence liquid and bring it somewhere else if you wish.
At the center of the spring's fountain is an unbreakable block which generates the essence liquid. If the block above it is air, it has a chance every tick to replace it with the fluid. This ensures you can't softlock yourself out of a stamina upgrade.

### Health Upgrades

Health has a total of **5** permanent upgrades.
This brings your base hearts from 5 to 10.

Health upgrades are obtained from varying sources.
#### Crystal Hearts
Crystal hearts are special blocks that generate in [[The Above]] as part of [[Crystal Trees]]. Each crystal tree has exactly one crystal heart visibly in the center of its trunk.
Eating a crystal heart for the first time gives you an extra maximum heart
#### Life Seed
A special heart-shaped seed that drops from the [[Void Blossom]] boss. You can plant it in the overworld to grow it into a giant flower-tree with a special jelly-like substance inside the flower bulb called **life jelly**. Eating life jelly for the first time gives you an extra maximum heart.
#### Gauntlet Item TBD
A special item that drops from the [[Gauntlet]] boss. TBD how it gives you an extra heart.
#### Malignant Heart
An item obtained from the sanctum. Related to sanguine armor. Eating it takes a chunk out of it, but that chunk grows back later. This item also acts as an infinite food source.
#### Ritual in the Beyond TBD
There's a structure that generates in the beyond where you can do some kind of TBD ritual that grants you an extra maximum heart. This can only be done once per player.

Each extra heart faintly resembles what created it. The crystal heart is kind of crystalline. The life jelly heart is kind of jelly-looking. The malignant heart looks all rough and fleshy.
This should be a subtle difference, not enough to make it stand out in the UI without close inspection.

### Overhealth Hearts

Another way to increase your hearts, albeit temporarily until the heart is damaged, is through **overhealth**. See [[Health & Damage Overhaul]] for more information.

There are exactly 3 possible overhealth hearts you can get, and they all come from structures you need to build.
#### Beds
Sleeping in a bed gives you 1 overhealth heart.
#### Hot Tubs
See [[Bubbles & Hot Tubs]] for more information.
#### TBD Animal
Feeding this animal fruit will make it grant you 1 overhealth heart. This has a cooldown equal to the breeding cooldown.

### Overstamina

Another way to increase your stamina, albeit temporarily until the stamina is spent, is through **overstamina**. See [[Stamina]] for more information.

There are exactly 3 possible overstamina bars you can get, and they all come from structures you need to build and interact with.
#### Beds
Sleeping in a bed gives you 1 overstamina bar.
#### Hot Tubs
See [[Bubbles & Hot Tubs]] for more information.
#### Splash Fruit
A special tree that grows glowing golden fruits full of juice. Generates rarely in [[Golden Woods & Wilds]] biomes.
Attempting to break one of the fruits makes it fall from the tree and burst into juice on collision with the ground, giving all players in the splash radius 1 overstamina bar.
