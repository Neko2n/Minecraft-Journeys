[[Progression]]

After descending through the scoria layer of the overworld, players will find themselves in the [[Upper Caverns]] of [[The Below]].

Escaping this progression tier has only one task. You must defeat [[The Wither]].

To spawn the wither, players must find a [[Wither Shrine]]. These generate as rooms in the bottom of nether fortresses, sealed behind a door that can only be opened with an **ancient key**. The room is protected by magic, so you won't be able to get in.

Ancient keys can be obtained from:
- Bastion chests
- Sanctum brazier chests
- Piglin trading
- Archaeology in the soul sand valley

Once you've gotten in, you'll find the wither's spawn structure with one of its heads already placed. Behind it is a nether brick statue of the wither with all three of its heads.

The wither spawner now requires a special soul sand block in the center containing the nether star. This can't be obtained in survival and only generates in these shrines as part of the spawner.

The 4 soul sand blocks that make up the wither spawner's body are protected by the nether star in the center and can't be broken. The protection spell overlay is identical to the wither's phase 2 overlay.

Once you've defeated the wither and obtained the nether star, give it to the [[Suspicious Trader]] in exchange for a [[Stronghold]] key.

Finally, players just need to go to [[The Beyond]] and travel to 0, 0 to the [[Stronghold]]. Deep inside, they'll find a portal to [[The Between]] sealed behind the stronghold key they bought from the trader.

Players will enter this portal and go into the final tier of their progression, [[Going Beyond]].
