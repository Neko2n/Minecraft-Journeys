[[Progression]]

After ascending to [[The Above]] from a [[Beacon Shrine]], players are now left with a choice between two major tasks to make it to the next stage of the game.

To get to [[The Below]], you need to be able to mine **scoria**, a new block that's like if deepslate, bedrock, and netherrack had a baby. It completely replaces the bedrock layer at the bottom of the overworld.

When you mine deep enough through the scoria at the bottom of the overworld, breaking a scoria block will create a rift. You can jump into this rift to fall into the below.

To break scoria, you'll need a pickaxe better than what you have available (iron/silver).
This requires you to obtain [[Mithril]].

There are two ways of getting mithril:
- Craft it at an altar (see [[Mithril]] for the recipe)
- Obtain it as loot from ominous vaults in [[Sentry Dungeons]]

While the loot route is more direct, obtaining an altar requires runestone, which is obtained exclusively from the [[Monolith]] boss.

Once players craft a mithril pickaxe and break through the overworld's scoria layer, they'll enter tier 3 of their progression, [[Going Below]].
