[[Early Game]]

### Knives & Spears

Knives and spears are new types of tools crafted with pebbles, bones, or flint.
There are also upgrades you can obtain with obsidian.

Knives act like shears on crops, allowing you to harvest plants directly.

Spears stick entities and items that they hit, allowing you to precisely lock down resources.
They work great as an alternative fishing tool.

There's a unique variant of the spear made purely with two sticks called the pike, which can thus be obtained before getting a crafting table.


### Wooden & Stone Tools

Stone tools are now crafted with pebbles at a crafting table.

Wooden tools are removed entirely.
