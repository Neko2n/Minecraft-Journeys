[[Progression]]

Early game is the first tier of progression.

Players start the game by first obtaining wood and pebbles (or flint/bones) to craft pikes, knives, and other basic items. They should focus on creating shelter before nightfall, else they should fall to the first of many [[Hunts]].

Players can craft a [[Workbench]] to gain access to recipes involving work stations, like the crafting table, furnace, and sawmill. The workbench is crafted with four wooden planks like a crafting table.

Once players obtain two cloth from zombies or dungeons, they can use the workbench to make a crafting table, giving them access to basic stone (or flint/bone) tools.

Once players can harvest stone and coal, they can use a workbench to create a furnace, allowing them to smelt copper and make copper tools.

Optionally, now that they have access to the crafting table, they can search out leather/cloth to make leather or cloth armor.

Players now must travel underground to find [[Thermal Shard]] items to have enough heat to smelt iron, allowing them to get iron tools.

Optionally, players may also use an [[Assembly Table]] to craft a mechanical press, which in turn can be powered to make metal plates, which make metal armor.

They then must collect at least 4 diamonds to activate a [[Beacon Shrine]].

Once they've successfully activated a beacon shrine, they'll enter tier 2 of their progression, [[Going Above]].
