[[Progression]]

Early game is the first tier of progression.

Players start the game by first obtaining wood and pebbles (or flint/bones) to craft pikes, knives, and other basic items. They should focus on creating shelter before nightfall, else they should fall to the first of many [[Hunts]].

Players can craft a [[Workbench]] to gain access to 2x2 grid recipes.

Once players obtain leather (requires engaging in husbandry), they can use the workbench to make a [[Toolbench]], giving them access to basic stone (or flint/bone) tools.

Once players can harvest stone and coal, they can use a workbench to create a furnace, allowing them to smelt copper and tin into ingots.

Players now must travel underground to find [[Thermite]] items to have enough heat to smelt iron. Once they have iron ingots and thermal shards, they can craft a [[Forge]] to unlock various crucial crafting recipes.

Players may also craft an [[Anvil]] and metal plates to upgrade leather armor into metal armors.

They then must collect at least 4 diamonds to activate a [[Beacon Shrine]].

Once they've successfully activated a beacon shrine, they'll enter tier 2 of their progression, [[Going Above]].
