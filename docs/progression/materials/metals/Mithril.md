[[Going Above]]

Mithril is a tier-2 resource made at an [[Altar]] with [[Silver]] and [[Diamond]].

Mithril is basically a replacement for the removed diamond tier of equipment.

You can upgrade existing silver gear into mithril gear by infusing it at an altar just like you would silver materials.

Mithril ingots look like light blue glowing ingots. They give off a faint animated glow in their sprite.

All mithril item and block textures are very faintly emmissive. The armor renders as faintly emmissive when worn.

Everything mithril-related gives off a small amount of light, with the items and equipment giving off slight dynamic light.

All mithril items and blocks emit a unique, barely-visible fog-like particle in the world that makes it look somewhat magical. The armor emits these particles while worn. The tools emit these particles when held out.

Obtaining a mithril pickaxe is required to complete the [[Going Above]] stage of progression, allowing you to mine through the scoria at the bottom of the overworld.

Mithril tools have reduced stamina costs and strengthened effects for [[Rune Abilities]]. They also have innate smite, dealing extra damage against undead enemies.

Mithril armor increases your stamina when worn, reduces magic damage, slows wither, and deals thorns damage to undead enemies. A full set of mithril armor makes you immune to wither entirely.
