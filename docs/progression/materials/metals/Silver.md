[[Going Above]]

Silver is a tier-2 material found exclusively in [[The Above]] that requires an iron pickaxe to mine, and requires hot heat to smelt (see: [[Smelting]])

It's about as rare in the above as diamonds are in the overworld. It generates in small veins of 1-4.

It is used in many recipes, with its primary use being various utility blocks and all magic weapons.

Silver's primary gimmick is magic.

Silver is required to make the [[Altar]], a key part of progression.
