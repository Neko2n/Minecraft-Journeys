[[Going Above]]

Platinum is a tier-2 resource that acts as the iron of [[The Above]]. It generates in really rare, but massive, veins that can stretch across many chunks. 

It is guaranteed to not generate within 200 blocks or so of a portal.

Platinum's primary usage is platinum tools and armor. It's also used as a general progression material, gating various "upgrade" recipes similarly to tin.

Platinum requires an iron pickaxe or above to mine.
