[[Early Game]]

Lead is an early-game metal which generates in boulder structures below deepslate and in the [[Upper Caverns]] of the below. These boulders are pretty much identical to amethyst geodes in rarity, shape, and size.

Lead requires hot heat to smelt (see: [[Smelting]])

Lead can be crafted into lead rods for numerous recipes which need a stronger metal stick instead of a nonsensically flimsy wooden stick.

Lead rods can be used to make **lead bolts**, which are stronger arrows that can only be fired from crossbows.

Lead rods are also used to make crossbows now, which are resprited to be made of lead.

Lead is used in all recipes which have to do with item transportation and storage.
