[[Early Game]]

Lead is an early-game metal which generates in large but rare boulder structures below deepslate and in the [[Upper Caverns]] of the below, similarly to iron.

Lead requires hot heat to smelt (see: [[Smelting]])

Lead can be crafted into lead rods for numerous recipes which need a stronger metal stick instead of a nonsensically flimsy wooden stick.

Lead rods can be used to make **lead bolts**, which are stronger arrows that can only be fired from crossbows.

Lead is used in all recipes which have to do with item transportation and storage, as well as any recipes which require a generic metal.
