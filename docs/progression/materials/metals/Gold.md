[[Early Game]]

Gold is an early-game metal used primarily for crafting healing items, building blocks, and cheap tools.

Gold is found in all three dimensions: the overworld, [[The Above]], and [[The Below]]. It is most plentiful in [[The Below]].

Like iron, gold requires hot heat to smelt. See: [[Smelting]]

Gold is the primary resource used to pacify and trade with [[Piglins]].
