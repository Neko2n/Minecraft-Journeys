[[Going Below]]

Soul Steel is a tier-3 metal made in a [[Crucible]] from alloying [[Iron]] and any coal variant with banshee powder.

Soul Steel's gimmick is [[Stamina]], making special use of essence orbs.

Soul Steel armor will sap the essence of mobs that hit you, making them drop essence orbs. The armor will sap 50% of collected essence orbs to temporarily increase the amount of armor it grants, but taking damage will drain your stamina scaling with how much the armor is powered up, as well as applying a little bit of stamina wither.

Soul Steel tools will sap 50% of collected essence orbs to temporarily increase the tool's mining speed, but mining drains your stamina scaling with how much the tool is powered up, as well as applying a little bit of stamina wither. Soul Steel tools also slowly power themselves up as you mine.

Soul Steel weapons will make enemies drop essence orbs when you hit them. They'll sap 50% of collected essence orbs to temporarily increase the weapon's swing speed and damage, but swinging the weapon will consume stamina scaling with how much the weapon is powered up, and will apply a little bit of stamina wither.

Soul steel shields make blocked attackers drop essence orbs, but each successful block withers your stamina a little.

The essence orbs dropped by soul steel uniquely cannot merge with other essence orbs. This is because they have special properties for PvP.
In PvP, soul steel will make opposing players lose stamina when they spawn essence orbs, and they cannot collect any of the essence orbs that they drop for 10 seconds.
