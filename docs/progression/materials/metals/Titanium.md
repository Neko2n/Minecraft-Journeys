[[Going Below]]

Titanium is a rare tier-3 metal which generates exclusively in the lower caverns of [[The Below]] in unique monolith structures, around lava layer (y60).

The monoliths it generates in are made of obsidian and *tremolite*, a new purple-ish stone faintly resembling bedrock.

Tremolite and titanium ore require a tier-3 tool to mine, but most tier-3 tools can only be obtained once you've gotten titanium, paradoxically.
The exception to this is mechanical drills. This needs to be obvious so players aren't stuck thinking they need to wait to get a better pickaxe they'll never be able to obtain.
Maybe have abandoned mine structures with bits of tremolite and titanium sitting next to broken mechanical drill setups and a blast furnace?

Titanium's primary use is making the [[Crucible]], but it's also used in the creation of many Create items in place of the now-removed sturdy sheets.

Titanium requires **blazing heat** to smelt. See: [[Smelting]]

The raw titanium sprite is very cubic with chunks of tremolite still attached.
The titanium ingot sprite is a perfect cube.

Titanium's primary color palette is purple.
