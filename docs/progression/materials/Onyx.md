[[Going Below]]

Onyx is a new gemstone resource that generates on unique drill-shaped structures made of scoria exclusively in the lower caverns of [[The Below]], jutting out of lava lakes and down from the ceilings.

The gemstone item is shaped like a drill bit.

Onyx's primary use is for making mining-related items.
It can make:
- Onyx arrows - breaks blocks that they hit. Attached to arrows in a fletching table.

Make a drill head in a crafting table with 3 onyx, 3 zinc, and 1 lead ingot.
Drill heads can make:
- Drill charges - travels forwards unaffected by gravity at a slow pace, drilling through all blocks in their path for up to 30 blocks or until they hit a block they can't mine. Crafted shapelessly with 1 fire charge and 1 drill head. Breaks blocks in a 3x3, with the outer blocks being broken at 50% speed, creating a drilling effect. The charge stops traveling when it hits a block until the block is fully mined, at which point it will continue traveling forward.
- Mechanical drill - the create item, resprited to look like it's made of onyx. Made in an assembly table with 1 drill head, 1 of any encased cogwheel, and 1 copper ingot
- **Onyx Drill** - made in a crafting table with 1 drill head, 1 electrum ingot, 1 thorium, and 4 tungsten ingots. See below.

### Onyx Drill

The onyx drill is a new "pickaxe" that mines like vanilla where it just drills linearly.
It's the strongest pickaxe in the game, being tier-3 and faster than all other pickaxes.

The onyx drill is uniquely two-handed, disabling your offhand while held.

The drill renders with a special 3D model in your hand, but the item sprite itself is still 2D.

You repair the onyx drill with onyx.

*This tool is only made possible due to the Incremental Mining mod and the Multi Mine mod working in tandem.*

### Onyx Drill - Charging

The onyx drill charges up between 3 charge stages the longer you hold down the mining button without releasing.
You can charge up the drill and maintain its charge even if you're not targeting a block.

Stage 1 is when you first start clicking. The drill spins and mines normally.
In stage 1, the drill mines at 50% of the speed of other tier-3 pickaxes.

Stage 2 activates after 3 seconds. The drill spins faster, and the head begins to faintly glow around the edges. The thorium core in the drill's handle starts glowing.
The drill mines twice as fast in stage 2 as it does in stage 1, putting it on equal footing with other tier-3 pickaxes.
The drill now deals rapid damage to mobs. It does no knockback.

Stage 3 activates after 10 seconds. The drill spins even faster, with the head going completely neon orange from how hot it is. The thorium core in the middle starts giving off fire and glows steadily.
While stage 3 is active, you'll passively accrue [[Heat]] at a rate of 0.25 heat per tick. This can reach 100, actually damaging you.
The drill once again mines twice as fast in stage 3, becoming the fastest tool in the game.
In stage 3, the drill automatically smelts mined blocks, so long as their smelting recipes require hot heat or lower.
In stage 3, the drill mines in a 3x3. The blocks at the edges are mined at 50% speed, creating a sort of drilling effect.
The drill deals twice as much damage to mobs as it did in stage 2, and now deals fire damage (doesn't actually set them on fire, though)

The onyx drill's [[Item Awakening]] gives it a fourth stage which activates after 15 seconds. The drill's head turns bright blue and emits fire. The tungsten core emits blue fire as well.
The drill is now 2x faster again, deals 2x more damage to mobs, and sets mobs on fire.
In stage 4, the drill mines in a 5x5. The blocks at the outer-most edges are mined at 25% speed, remaining consistent with the drilling effect.
In stage 4, he drill auto-smelts all mined blocks regardless of their heat requirements.
The amount of [[Heat]] you accrue in stage 4 is doubled, accruing 0.5 heat per tick.

Awakening the onyx drill also makes it reach stage 2 after 2 seconds instead of 3, and it reaches stage 3 after 7 seconds instead of 10.

### Onyx Drill - Fuel

The onyx drill has a unique "fuel" system where it accepts [[Thorium]] as if it was a bundle. You can put up to 64 thorium in the drill.

While holding out the onyx drill, a little thorium item icon and a number renders next to your hotbar, in the same place your ammunition for ranged weapons renders.
While actively drilling, this item icon lights up and emits fire. With each stage, the icon gets brighter. In stage 4, the icon turns bright blue.

While actively drilling in stages 2-4, a little thorium icon appears below your cursor that slowly fills up. Each time it caps out, the thorium in the drill is reduced by 1.
The icon fills up 2x faster with each stage. At stage 2 it takes 8 seconds to max out the icon. At stage 4, it takes 2 seconds.

If the drill has no thorium in it, it will be locked at stage 1 and cannot charge up.
