[[Early Game]]

Hardstone is a crucial block resource found alongside diamond ore. Diamond ore is inside of hardstone.

Hardstone requires an iron pickaxe or stronger to mine.

Hardstone is crafted with 1 diamond dust and 3 of any sturdy stone (deepslate, granite, etc.) in a forge. This makes 1 hardstone.

Diamond dust is made by crushing a diamond in a mortar. 1 diamond makes 3-4 diamond dust.
