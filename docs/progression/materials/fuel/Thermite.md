[[Early Game]]

Thermite is a unique progression item used to craft and fuel the [[Forge]], the [[Crucible]], and the brewing stand.

They drop from Deepers and Evendeepers. Deepers drop 1, while Evendeepers drop 2-3.

They can also be found in dungeon loot underground, and obtained from rare ore blocks found in gravel veins. These ore blocks explode into fire when mined without a [[Chisel]], or when they fall and land. These are re-skinned flint blocks from caverns and chasms.

Thermite has a fuel value of 2 and smelts at a hot temperature. See: [[Smelting]]

If a thermite item entity catches on fire, it will set the block it is on on fire.

The thermite item is basically just re-named blaze powder.

You can combine coal/charcoal and thermite together in your inventory to make **coke coal**, which has a fuel value of 8 and smelts at a hot temperature.
