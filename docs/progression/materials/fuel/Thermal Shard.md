[[Early Game]]

Thermal shards are a unique progression item used to craft and fuel the [[Forge]], the [[Crucible]], and the brewing stand.

They drop from Deepers and Evendeepers. Deepers drop 1, while Evendeepers drop 2-3.

They can also be found in dungeon loot underground, and obtained from rare ore blocks.

Thermal shards have a fuel value of 16, twice as much as coal, and smelt at a hot temperature. See: [[Smelting]]

**Incendiary Items**
If a thermal shard item entity catches on fire, it will set the block it is on on fire.
If a thermal shard item entity contains multiple thermal shards in its stack, it will create a small explosion.
The explosion scales with the number of shards in the stack. At 2 thermal shards, this will just be a little pop of fire particles. At 64 thermal shards, the explosion will be 4x bigger than a deeper explosion.

**Sprite**
Thermal shards resemble amethyst shards, but have two shards instead of just one.
The sprite is animated with a soft, pulsing glow.
