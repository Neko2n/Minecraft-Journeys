Being near any campfire grants a unique effect called cozy with a campfire sprite. Doubles the acceleration of stamina regeneration when standing still. Requires you to be within a 4 block radius of the campfire.

UI is a campfire icon above your stamina bar with a green bobbing up arrow. The bobbing arrow only displays when you're standing still.

Later in the game, you can use blaze rods to make a "Heater" block. Activating it with redstone grants the cozy effect to all players in a 16 block radius.
