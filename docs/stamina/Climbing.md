[[Stamina]]

Walk up to a block face with air above you and jump while continuing to walk into it to begin climbing.

Climbing costs stamina, and running out of stamina will detach you and send you falling down.

While climbing, your hotbar is greyed out and you cannot use items.

Some blocks are faster and don't require stamina to climb. This is like ladders, vines, etc.
Some blocks are faster to climb. This is any rough block like cobblestone, wood, logs, bricks, etc.
Some blocks are slower to climb. This is smooth blocks like stone,  etc.
Some blocks can't be climbed at all. This is any wet block, ice, etc.
When exposed to a certain level of skylight during rain, climbing takes twice as much stamina, shown by a little water droplet icon next to your stamina wheel.

When you hit a ledge, your character hoists itself up on top of the block.

You can climb sideways, including around block faces.
