[[Going Below]]

Sanguine is a material that applies exclusively to the sanguine armor set from Caverns and Chasms, which has been completely reworked.

Sanguine armor is not craftable, and can only be found in Sanctum brazier chests.

Sanguine armor builds up "blood" when you pick up essence orbs. At maximum blood, the armor will heal you by 1 heart before resetting its blood back to zero again.
The amount of blood gained from essence orbs scales with the number of pieces of sanguine armor you have equipped.
The blood meter renders on the left-hand side of your screen as a little red vertical meter with the sanguine helmet item icon at the top.

Sanguine armor cannot be repaired, and instead regenerates its own durability over time naturally.

When sanguine armor runs out of durability, it doesn't die, but instead becomes "withered" until its durability has regenerated to at least 50%. Withered sanguine armor does not provide the blood effect, but can still be worn as armor; just be aware that it still loses durability, so if you keep using it, it won't be able to get back to 50%.
