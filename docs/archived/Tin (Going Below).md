[[Going Below]]

Tin is a new material found exclusively in the [[Upper Caverns]] of the below. It's found specifically in monoliths that generate akin to amethyst geodes, making it rarer than other ores until you find it for the first time.

Smelting tin requires blazing heat. See: [[Smelting]]

Tin can be used to make tinplate, an alternative to honeycomb which can coat blocks to prevent them from oxidizing *and* make the blocks fireproof.
Tinplate is made in a shapeless crafting recipe with 1 tin ingot and 1 zinc ingot, making 4 tinplate.

Tin is primarily used to make upgraded storage blocks & items, as well as some unique redstone components. These are mostly unchanged features from Caverns and Chasms.

All tin-related items and blocks are fireproof.

Tin is also used to make [[Blaze Bronze]], a new fiery weapon/armor material.
