**Variant Recipes**
Make variant campfires 1 regular campfire + a catalyst material
Make variant torches 1 regular torch + a catalyst material
Make variant lanterns 1 regular lantern + a catalyst material
Catalyst materials are as follows:
- Cupric - **Patina**, new green dust dropped from scraping oxidized copper blocks
- Glowing - Glowstone Dust
- Soul - **Soul Sand**, in its new item form (see: [[Consistent Dust Blocks]])

**Rebalanced Campfire Drops**
Change campfires to drop 1 charcoal and 1-2 supplementaries ash.
